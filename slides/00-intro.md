# What is a Staff Engineer?

- The level beyond Senior where you're **no longer bound to a single team**
- Technical leadership **without direct reports**
- Lead through influence, technical direction, and expertise

<!--
This is where the individual contributor career path diverges from management. You're still an individual contributor, but your scope and impact has expanded dramatically. You're afforded autonomy and access that means you can have an impact across a much larger radius of your organization.
-->

---

# Impact Radius

| Level | Scope |
|-------|-------|
| **Junior** | Task level |
| **Mid** | Feature level |
| **Senior** | Team level, stretching towards zone |
| **Staff** | Zone level or beyond |
| **Principal** | Multi-zone, multiple horizons into the future |
| **Distinguished** | Company-wide impact, long-term strategic thinking |

The minimum bar for Staff is **zone-level or organizational impact**.

<!--
The impact radius is one of the key differentiator between individual contributor levels. Staff is the first level where you're **expected** to have impact beyond your immediate team.
At earlier levels you still might have influence at a zone level, but the expectation is what sets the roles apart.
-->

---

# Autonomy at Staff Level

- Identify and discover high-impact problems worth solving
- Manage their own time budget
- Apply themselves where they'll be most effective
- Operate without needing direction on what to work on
- Impact measured in outcomes, not contributions

<!--
At Staff level, no one is assigning you work. You're expected to find the most important problems and solve them.
You need to go hunting for high impact problems and optimize your time and attention to address them. To correctly identify these opportunities you need to have business awareness along with the technical and an understanding of what the company wants to prioritise.
-->

---

# Staff Engineer vs. Engineering Manager

Staff engineers may lead teams on specific projects, but they are **not** Engineering Managers.

| Staff Engineer | Engineering Manager |
|----------------|---------------------|
| No performance reviews | Owns career development & evaluation |
| No hiring authority | Manages headcount & hiring |
| No admin overhead | Budgets, timecards, HR processes |
| Mentoring-focused 1:1s | Personnel management 1:1s |

<!--
Without question this is a leadership role, and though at times you will have people turning to you for direction and you will be helping engineers grow into their careers, it's very distinct from being a manager.
At the end of the day you're still responsible for your individual contributions to the org rather than organizing the contribution of others. While you will be responsible for improving the contribution and effectiveness of others, and you will be responsible for leading projects, you're not responsible for those individuals. It's an important distinction to keep in mind when thinking about the leadership style and tools available to both of these roles.
-->

# A Note on Terminology

Different companies define "Staff Engineer" differently:

- **Our definition**: First level beyond Senior where you operate across teams with zone/org-level impact

- **Some companies**: Staff is a "super senior" on a single team, only graduating to Principal+ for cross-team work

Throughout this presentation, we use the first definition.
